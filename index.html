About & Leadership

Shaping the Future of Youth-Focused Digital Media in India.

Founded with a bold vision to cut through digital noise, News Teen75 combines clean product design with rapid editorial curation to keep India’s next generation informed.

Our Story

Born for the Smartphone Generation. Built for Credibility.

Traditional news portals were built for desktop browsers—cluttered with pop-ups, slow-loading pages, and fragmented navigation. We recognized that today’s readers consume news on the move and demand both speed and visual clarity.

News Teen75 was established to redefine how national events, business shifts, technology breakthroughs, and youth-relevant stories are experienced. By organizing our mobile ecosystem into intuitive verticals (For You, India, Business, Tech), we empower readers to stay informed in seconds while giving brand partners a high-attention canvas.

Our Mission

To deliver verified, high-velocity news in a zero-clutter interface tailored for India’s young citizens and professionals.

Our Vision

To become India’s most trusted mobile news network and the premier digital storytelling partner for modern brands.
